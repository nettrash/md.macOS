md is a Markdown editor and live previewer built for the Mac. Write plain Markdown on one side and watch the finished page take shape on the other - then print it, export it, or turn a folder of chapters into a whole book.

WRITE AND SEE
- Edit, Split and Preview layouts (Command-1, 2 and 3). In Split, the two panes scroll as one.
- Zen mode (Shift-Command-Return): full screen, your text in one centred column, for writing or for reading.
- The preview is the printed page: one renderer draws the preview, the printout and the PDF.
- Headings, lists, task lists, tables, quotes, code, links and images - plus footnotes, and front matter (YAML or TOML) kept out of the page.
- LaTeX math and mhchem chemistry, Mermaid graphs, Graphviz and PlantUML diagrams - all drawn on your Mac by engines built into the app, and charts from a plot block.
- Fenced code is highlighted in a quiet palette.
- Paste a table from a spreadsheet into a csv or tsv block and md draws it, numbers lined up on the right.
- The Go menu jumps to any heading, and lists your private notes - an HTML comment on its own line whose text begins with note - which never appear in the preview, the PDF or the printout.
- A live word and character count sits under every page.

FIND, FORMAT AND TYPE
- A find bar over your source (Command-F, Option-Command-F for Replace), with Replace All as a single undo.
- A Format menu: bold, italic, code, strikethrough, links, headings, lists, task lists, quotes and indents, each on a shortcut.
- Return continues lists, quotes and tables, and the first letter of each line and sentence is capitalized as you type. Both are switches under Edit, Typing.

PRINT AND EXPORT
- Print or export real A4 pages - or A5, US Letter, US Legal, or a paperback trim size. Breaks fall between lines, and \newpage starts a fresh page wherever you decide.
- Export as HTML: one self-contained file that opens anywhere, diagrams as drawings and formulas as real, selectable text.
- Export as LaTeX: a .tex file where your mathematics stays mathematics.
- Export a diagram or a plot as a standalone .svg vector, or the document as a standard EPUB 3, with its author, language, series and cover taken from the front matter.
- Share the rendered PDF, or open and export TextBundle and TextPack.

WRITER MODE: BOOKS
- A book is simply a folder: its subfolders are chapters, its Markdown files are articles, ordered by name.
- The book window keeps the structure in a slim sidebar and the article you are writing beside it - Edit, Split or Preview - saving as you type.
- Walk the manuscript from the keyboard (Control-Command-Up and Down). Rename, reorder and delete chapters and articles in place; the order is written back to the file names, so it stays true in the Finder.
- Compile the whole book into one PDF at the page size you choose, print it, or export it as a standard EPUB 3 with a proper table of contents - math and diagrams travel with it as images.

A REAL MAC APP
- Document-based: open, save, autosave, versions, rename, duplicate and move come from macOS itself. Your files stay ordinary text - .md, .markdown and every other Markdown spelling, or .txt - in the encoding they arrived in; md also opens PlantUML, Graphviz, TextBundle and TextPack files.
- Quick Look: press Space on a Markdown file in the Finder to see it rendered, without opening it.
- Built-in examples, including a small sample book, show everything the app can do.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.
- Your documents stay where you put them: on your Mac, or in your own iCloud Drive.
- Everything is rendered on your Mac. md fetches only what you name by URL: an image in your document, or the cover of an EPUB you export.

Written in Swift and SwiftUI. The only outside code is the open-source engines - KaTeX, mhchem, Mermaid, Graphviz, PlantUML and highlight.js - bundled in and run on your Mac. The source is public.
