md is a Markdown editor and live previewer built for the Mac. Write plain Markdown on one side and watch the finished page take shape on the other - then print it, export it as a PDF, or turn a folder of chapters into a whole book.

WRITE AND SEE
- Edit, Split and Preview layouts (Command-1 / Command-2 / Command-3). In Split, the two panes scroll as one, so the passage you are reading is the paragraph you are writing.
- The preview is the printed page: one renderer draws the preview, the printout and the PDF - the same layout, set for paper (white pages, dark ink, print-sized type).
- Headings, lists, task lists, tables, quotes, code blocks, links and images - plus LaTeX math, Mermaid graphs and PlantUML diagrams, all drawn on your Mac by engines built into the app.
- The Go menu lists every heading and jumps both panes to it.
- Private author notes - an HTML comment on its own line whose text begins with "note:" - are listed in the Go menu and never appear in the preview, the PDF or the printout.
- A live word and character count sits under every page.

PRINT AND EXPORT
- Print or export real A4 pages. Breaks fall between lines, never through them, and \newpage starts a fresh page wherever you decide.
- Share the rendered PDF from any window, or export it through the standard save panel.

WRITER MODE: BOOKS
- A book is simply a folder: its subfolders are chapters, its Markdown files are articles, ordered by name.
- The book window keeps the structure in a slim sidebar and the article you are writing beside it - Edit, Split or Preview - saving as you type. Take it full screen for a writing room with nothing in it but your book.
- Walk the manuscript from the keyboard (Control-Command-Up / Control-Command-Down). Rename, reorder and delete chapters and articles in place; the order is written back to the file names, so it stays true in the Finder.
- Compile the whole book into one PDF, print it, or export it as a standard EPUB 3 with a proper table of contents - math and diagrams travel with it as images.

A REAL MAC APP
- Document-based: open, save, autosave, versions, rename, duplicate and move come from macOS itself. Your files stay ordinary .md, .markdown or .txt files, in the encoding they arrived in.
- Native menus, keyboard shortcuts and full screen throughout - no toolbar clutter.
- Built-in examples, including a small sample book, show everything the app can do.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.
- Your documents stay where you put them: on your Mac, or in your own iCloud Drive.
- Math and diagrams are rendered on your Mac. The only thing md fetches from the network is an image your own document points at by URL.

Written in Swift and SwiftUI, with no accounts and no servers. The only outside code in the app is the open-source engines that draw the math and the diagrams - KaTeX, Mermaid, Graphviz and PlantUML - bundled in, and run on your Mac. The source code is public.
